# Cross-Stage Decision Ledger

A compact index of every significant technical decision, with its current evidential status and **what would cause us to reverse it**.

This ledger is a *navigation aid*. The full argument for each decision lives in `docs/architecture_decisions.md`; it is not duplicated here.

**Status vocabulary** (same as the ADR register):
`ACCEPTED` — validated by experiment · `PROPOSED` — reasoned but unvalidated · `SUPERSEDED` · `REJECTED`

**A decision is not `ACCEPTED` merely because it has been implemented.** Six of the twelve ADRs remain `PROPOSED` despite being fully built and in daily use, because the experiment that would validate them has not run.

## Proposed — reasoned, implemented, but **not yet validated**

| **D-001** (ADR-0001) | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | Fixed geometric protocol with a **swappable matcher engine**; do not select the matcher yet | (a) commit to one learned matcher; (b) purely classical; (c) ensemble | SAR-optical benchmark: protocol choices change mean error **up to 33×**, exceeding the top-vs-mid matcher gap (sources.md S4) | If it transfers, effort belongs on the dominant axis — and those stages are CPU-cheap | **PROPOSED** | **Accept** if EXP-006 reproduces the protocol-dominance effect on lunar data. **Reverse** if matcher choice dominates protocol there. | **D-002** (ADR-0002) | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | **CPU-first**; GPU optional, never a runtime dependency of the deliverable | (a) require a GPU; (b) rent cloud GPU; (c) classical only | Measured: no CUDA, 4C/8T, 15.2 GB (RL-001). Published GPU latency: XoFTR 0.4 s vs RoMa 5.0 s/pair | A deliverable requiring hardware we lack is not a deliverable (spec §56) | **PROPOSED** | **Accept** when EXP-005 measures actual CPU latency per engine. Option (c) was rejected for foreclosing engines before measuring them |
| **D-004** (ADR-0004) ~~PROPOSED~~ **SUPERSEDED by EXP-003 (RL-023)** | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | ~~Default matching representation is polarity-agnostic structure~~ — **refuted: mod-π was the worst of six arms (wins 0, loses 38 vs its own 2π control on identical keypoints); raw intensity won** | (a) raw intensity; (b) CLAHE/histogram matching; (c) rely on learned invariance; (d) physical relighting | Structural: SIFT/ORB/AKAZE bin gradient orientation over `[0, 2π)`; contrast normalisation is sign-preserving and cannot repair a sign flip. Measured: image gradients anti-correlate at **−0.853** under azimuth reversal | Prefer invariance provable by construction over invariance merely trained and hoped for. **The invariance was provable and was not enough:** phase congruency is contrast- and polarity-invariant to 1e-6 / exactly 0.0 and still stops at 27–30°, because shadow motion changes *which structures exist*, not their contrast | **SUPERSEDED** (2026-08-24, EXP-003) | **Already reversed.** EXP-003 met the ADR's own supersession condition: raw intensity won. Reinstate only if a polarity-agnostic representation is shown to beat raw intensity on a realistic A-regime under a pre-registered criterion |
| **D-005** (ADR-0005) | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | **Scale normalisation is a required pipeline stage**, not preprocessing | (a) rely on the matcher's scale invariance; (b) multi-scale matching without normalisation; (c) require metadata | Published GSDs give pairwise ratios 2:1 … **320:1** (RL-002, sources.md S1/S2); off-the-shelf matchers are reliable to ~2–4× | Option (a) is refuted by arithmetic. Option (c) is refused by spec §24 (must degrade without metadata) | **PROPOSED** | **Accept** when EXP-004 sweeps the real ladder and shows where normalisation stops rescuing the match. | **D-006** (ADR-0006) | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | **`max_uncovered_disc_radius`** is the primary coverage metric; occupancy, entropy, hull ratio secondary | (a) grid occupancy; (b) spatial entropy; (c) convex hull ratio | Argument from what the metric *bounds*: registration error grows with distance to the nearest constraint, so the largest empty disc upper-bounds worst-case local error. Averages cannot bound a worst case | Spec §15 asks which metric is most meaningful **and for the justification** | **PROPOSED** | **Accept** if EXP-007 shows it correlates with local held-out error better than the alternatives. **Reverse** if it does not — then it is simply the wrong metric. Supporting anecdote only: it flagged a correct-but-fragile 8-inlier case that error metrics called a success |
| **D-008** (ADR-0008) | 2026-08-24 | PHASE-0 | **Licence audit gates every pretrained weight**; prefer Apache-2.0 / MIT | (a) use best weights, address licensing later; (b) avoid pretrained weights entirely | Risk R7. SuperGlue's weights are *believed* research-/non-commercial-only — **recorded as UNVERIFIED**, to be checked against the actual licence text | A licence problem found at evaluation is unrecoverable; choosing permissively early costs nothing | **PROPOSED** | **Accept** once the audit table exists and is populated. Currently no weights are in use, so nothing is blocked |
